Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your KAIRO account and data, as a customer or as a service partner.

Applies to KAIRO — User App and Partner App (Android & iOS)

Option 1 — Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open the KAIRO app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account (or Settings → Delete Account).
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account is deleted immediately once confirmed — this action can't be undone from within the app.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app (lost your phone number, uninstalled the app, etc.), email us and we'll delete your account manually.

Emailkairoservicess@gmail.com

If you have an active booking or a pending wallet payout, complete or withdraw it before requesting deletion.
