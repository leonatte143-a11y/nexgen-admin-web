01 Scope

KAIRO ("KAIRO", "we", "us") operates an on-demand home services marketplace connecting customers with independent service partners (electricians, plumbers, cleaners, and similar professionals), plus a partner-facing app and an internal admin panel used by our operations team. This policy explains what information we collect through the KAIRO customer app, the KAIRO partner app, and associated web tools, and how that information is handled.

By creating an account or using KAIRO, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

02 Information we collect

What we collect depends on whether you're using KAIRO as a customer or as a service partner.

03 How we use it

  • Create and secure your account, and verify your identity via OTP.
  • Match customers with nearby, available, verified partners.
  • Process bookings, payments, refunds, and partner payouts.
  • Send booking updates, receipts, and service reminders.
  • Provide customer support and investigate disputes or safety reports.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and policy violations.
  • Improve app reliability and features based on aggregated usage patterns.
  • Send offers or promotions — you can opt out of marketing messages at any time in-app.

04 How we share it

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only where a booking or the service itself requires it:

  • Between customers and partners — a partner accepting your booking sees your name, address, and contact number needed to complete the job; a customer sees the assigned partner's name, photo, and rating.
  • Payment processors — to complete transactions and payouts securely.
  • Service providers we rely on — cloud hosting, SMS/notification delivery, and analytics providers, bound by confidentiality obligations, only to operate KAIRO on our behalf.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — only when legally required, or to protect the safety of our users.

05 Retention

We keep account and booking data for as long as your account is active, plus a limited period afterward to meet accounting, tax, dispute-resolution, and fraud-prevention obligations. Partner KYC documents are retained for the period required by applicable regulation. You can request account deletion at any time (see Section 07); some records may be retained where the law requires it even after deletion.

06 Security

OTP codes are never stored in plain text — they're hashed before being saved, and expire within minutes. Access to customer and partner data within KAIRO is restricted to authorized staff on a need-to-know basis, gated by role-based permissions in our admin systems. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard safeguards.

07 Your choices

  • Access & update — review and edit your profile information directly in the app.
  • Delete your account — request account and data deletion from the app's profile settings, or by contacting us (see Section 10).
  • Marketing preferences — opt out of promotional notifications at any time; you'll still receive essential booking-related messages.
  • Location — you can disable location permissions in your device settings, though this will limit KAIRO's ability to match you with nearby partners.

08 Children

KAIRO is intended for users aged 18 and over, as booking and paying for home services requires legal capacity to enter into a contract. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

09 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as KAIRO's features evolve. Material changes will be notified in-app or by other reasonable means before they take effect. The "Effective" date at the top of this page always reflects the latest version.
